03 / Decision priorities

Balance fuel price benefits with performance, reliability, safety and environmental compliance.

↗

Blend optimization

Run controlled blend trials and compare steam output, heat rate, bed temperature and auxiliary consumption at comparable loads.

⚙

Feeder reliability

Track choking frequency, stoppage duration, motor current and manual cleaning hours by feeder and blend ratio.

◉

Performance verification

Review coal GCV, moisture, ash and sulphur with emissions, limestone consumption and ash-handling records.

＋

Net cost assessment

Include transport, handling, efficiency changes, limestone, ash disposal, maintenance and downtime before reporting net savings.

✓

Safe operation

Use approved operating limits and risk controls. Do not change feeder settings or operating limits without site authorization.

▤

Management reporting

Maintain a daily register for blend ratio, coal consumption, feeder issues, operating trends, actions and accountable owners.

Calculation limitation: Estimated differential = (RB-3 price − Thar coal price) × Thar coal quantity. It assumes the entered Thar quantity replaces an equal quantity of reference coal and excludes differences in calorific value, transport, efficiency, consumables, maintenance and downtime. Validate all figures before using them for financial or management decisions.
